About Fixam

Fixam bridges the gap between citizens and local government by providing an accessible, low-barrier channel for reporting infrastructure problems. Anyone with WhatsApp can report a broken streetlight, pothole, water leak, or waste pile-up in seconds.

Designed as a Digital Public Good (DPG), Fixam is free, open-source, and built to serve communities — particularly in developing nations where traditional civic reporting channels are limited.

Features

📱

WhatsApp Bot

Citizens report issues via WhatsApp using natural language. The bot guides them through category selection, location sharing, and photo evidence — no app install required.

🗺️

Interactive Civic Map

All reported issues appear on a public map with real-time status tracking. Filter by category, location, or status to see what's happening in your community.

🤖

AI Intent Classification

Natural language processing understands citizen intents — reporting, voting, tracking, or feedback — making interactions feel conversational and intuitive.

🗳️

Community Voting

Citizens upvote and downvote issues to help authorities prioritize repairs. Popular issues rise to the top for faster resolution.

🏆

Gamification

Points and leaderboards reward active citizens. Earn points for reporting, voting, and endorsing resolutions — building a culture of civic pride.

🔒

Privacy-First

Explicit consent required before data collection. bcrypt password hashing, restricted CORS, HTTPS enforcement, and full data export/deletion rights.

Open Source

Fixam is proudly open source under the ISC License. We believe civic infrastructure tools should be freely available to all communities worldwide.

The full codebase is available on GitHub, including:

  • WhatsApp bot integration with conversation state machine
  • AI service for intent classification and content moderation
  • PostgreSQL database with full schema and migrations
  • Admin dashboard for issue management and analytics
  • Public civic map and statistics dashboard
