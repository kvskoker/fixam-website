2. What Fixam Is

Fixam is a civic technology platform built for Sierra Leone. It lets citizens report local infrastructure problems — broken roads, faulty streetlights, water supply failures, waste pile-ups, and similar issues — over WhatsApp. Reports are classified, mapped, and shared with the relevant authorities to help them prioritise and track repairs.

Fixam is not a government agency and not an emergency service. We pass reports to the responsible authorities, but we do not carry out repairs and cannot guarantee that any report will be acted upon. For emergencies — fire, medical, crime, or immediate danger to life — contact the emergency services directly. Do not rely on Fixam.
